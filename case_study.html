Overview

This project rebrands the discontinued 90s juice brand "Squeezit" for today's consumers. I handled everything from planning and branding to web coding, creating a new logo, a sustainable label free bottle, a promotional website, and a brand story video based on three traits: Delightful, Playful, and Boosting.

Challenge & Research

The goal was to bring a discontinued juice brand back to life as a vitamin rich tropical fruit juice made with lemon, mango, coconut, and orange. Inspired by the name "Squeezit" and its soft plastic bottle, I focused on combining a fun, squeezing experience with eco-friendly packaging to appeal to modern consumers.

Creative Process

Starting from the brand name, I branched out into three core brand personality

  • Delightful: Bright tropical flavors that bring joy and vibrant energy.
  • Playful: A soft, squeezable bottle that turns drinking into a fun, interactive moment.
  • Boosting: Vitamin-enhanced fruit juice that gives a natural energy lift with every squeeze.

For sustainability, I designed a label-free bottle without extra paper or plastic wraps. Product details are printed directly onto the bottle using washable ink that easily washes off during the recycling process, reducing waste and making sorting simple.

Final Solution

For the logo, I shaped the letter "i" like a squeezed bottle with a splashing drop, and added water-splash marks around the letters to capture the playful squeezing action. On the website, using only bright colors could look too light, so I balanced the layout with clean white space, soft gradient backgrounds, rounded fonts, and flexible frames. I also created a kitschy, delightful brand story video where a tired character goes through a tough journey, drinks Squeezit, and bursts with joy.

Reflection

Completing this entire project from brand strategy and bottle design to motion graphics and front-end coding taught me how to connect different media into one consistent brand story. I learned how to balance bright, playful themes with white space and soft gradients for a polished look, and how to merge fun user interaction with sustainable packaging design.
